Speech

AdditionalProperties#
map<any>
input#
*string

Text to generate a speech from

metadata#
map<any>|null
model#
string|null
prompt_cache_key#
string|null
ref_audio#
string|null

The audio reference for generating the speech.

response_format#
"pcm"|"wav"|"mp3"|"flac"|"opus"
stream#
boolean

Default Value: false

voice_id#
string|null

The preset or custom voice to use for generating the speech.

Speech audio data.

*string
