## Upcoming Gen2 Streaming Deprecation

Our legacy Gen2 streaming model is set to be deprecated on 16th August 2026. We recommend migrating to our latest [Falcon 2](/api/docs/text-to-speech-models/falcon-2) model promptly.

Gen2 non-streaming text-to-speech will remain available through the [Synthesize Speech](/api/docs/api-reference/text-to-speech/generate) endpoint.
